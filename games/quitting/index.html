Variant 5: Cumulative reward, risk of ruin

This one isn't about picking the best from a list — it's a run of risky rounds. Every success adds to your winnings, but every round also carries a chance of failure, and one failure loses everything you've built up.

Early on you have little to lose, so playing on makes sense. As your pile grows, each extra round risks more of it. The best moment to stop is when the expected gain from one more round no longer outweighs the risk of losing it all.

  1. Estimate your chance of success and your chance of failure each round.
  2. Divide the chance of success by the chance of failure.
  3. That's roughly how many successful rounds to aim for.
  4. Once you reach that number, stop and keep your winnings.
